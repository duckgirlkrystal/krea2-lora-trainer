When training finishes, this folder will contain:

  yourname_krea2_000001000.safetensors   <- your LoRA file
  preview_images\                        <- pictures it made

You may see several .safetensors files with different numbers.
Those are snapshots taken at different points during training.

The biggest number is NOT always the best one. A LoRA can be
"overtrained", where it copies the photos too literally and stops
being flexible. Start with the one around 1000 and compare.

The training_log.txt file in here is just a record of what
happened. You can ignore it unless something went wrong.
