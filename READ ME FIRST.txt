====================================================================
  KRYSTAL'S LORA TRAINER
  Teach an image generator what one specific person looks like.
====================================================================


WHAT THIS DOES
--------------

You give it 20-50 photos of one person. It gives you back a small
file called a "LoRA", plus some preview pictures so you can see it
worked.

That LoRA file is what you load into an image generator later to
make new pictures of that person.

The whole thing takes about 1 to 2 hours. You do not have to sit
and watch it.


====================================================================
  STEP 1 - PUT YOUR PHOTOS IN THE "my_photos" FOLDER
====================================================================

Open the folder called "my_photos" and drop the pictures in.

  - At least 15 photos. 25 to 40 works best.
  - All of the SAME person.
  - Mix them up. Close-up faces, waist-up shots, full body.
    Different outfits, different lighting, different places.
  - Sharp and in focus. Blurry photos make the result worse.
  - Try to avoid other people being in the shot.
  - iPhone photos (.HEIC) are fine. They get converted for you.

More photos is not automatically better. 30 good photos beat
100 mediocre ones.


====================================================================
  STEP 2 - PICK HOW YOU WANT TO TRAIN
====================================================================

There are two ways. Pick one.


  OPTION A - RENT A CLOUD COMPUTER  (easiest, costs about $3)
  -----------------------------------------------------------

      Double-click:   1 - TRAIN IN THE CLOUD.bat

  Works on ANY computer, even an old laptop with no graphics card.
  It rents a powerful computer for an hour or two, trains your
  character on it, downloads the result, and then shuts the rented
  computer down so you stop paying.

  You need a RunPod account with some money on it. The program
  tells you exactly how to get one.


  OPTION B - USE YOUR OWN GRAPHICS CARD  (free, but picky)
  ---------------------------------------------------------

      Double-click:   2 - TRAIN ON MY PC.bat

  You need:
    - An NVIDIA graphics card with 16GB of memory or more.
      (RTX 3090, 4080, 4090, 5080, 5090, and similar.)
    - 60GB of free disk space.
    - Ideally 64GB of system RAM. 32GB can work but needs a
      settings change the program will explain.

  The program checks all of this for you first and tells you
  plainly if your PC can't handle it. Nothing breaks if it can't.


Either way, the program will ask you a few simple questions the
first time you run it, and remember your answers after that.

You do NOT need to edit any files. Just double-click and read.


====================================================================
  ABOUT MONEY (only matters for Option A, the cloud)
====================================================================

Renting the cloud computer costs roughly $1.40 per hour, and a
normal run takes about 1.5 hours. So about $2 to $3 per character.

Please do these two things before your first cloud run:

  1. Put about $30 on your RunPod account. Not more.
     RunPod is prepaid. If there is only $30 on the account, $30
     is the absolute most you can ever be charged. This is the
     single best protection against a surprise bill.

  2. Turn OFF auto-pay in your RunPod account settings.
     Auto-pay defeats the protection above.

The program is built to shut the rented computer down when it is
finished, and has several independent backstops in case something
crashes. But nothing beats a spending cap.

IF YOU ARE EVER WORRIED that something is still running and
costing money:

      Double-click:   3 - EMERGENCY - SHUT DOWN CLOUD.bat

That shuts down every computer this program rented. If you happen
to run other things on the same RunPod account, it lists those
separately and asks first, so it will not kill your own work.
It is always safe to run. Run it any time you feel unsure.

You can also always go to https://console.runpod.io/pods in a
browser and delete anything listed there by hand.


====================================================================
  WHERE YOUR RESULTS GO
====================================================================

Everything lands in the "output" folder.

  - A file ending in .safetensors        <- this is your LoRA
  - A "preview_images" folder            <- proof it worked

You do NOT have to wait until the end to look. Each snapshot and
preview picture is copied into that folder as soon as it is ready,
roughly every few minutes, and the window tells you each time one
arrives. Open them whenever you like while the rest carries on.

If several .safetensors files show up, they are snapshots from
different points in training. Later is not always better. Try the
one around step 1000 first, then compare.


====================================================================
  IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
====================================================================

The program tries to explain problems in plain English rather than
showing you a wall of red text. If you hit something confusing,
take a screenshot of the window and send it over.

The window stays open when there is an error so you can read it.
Do not worry about breaking anything. You cannot damage your
computer by re-running this, and re-running after a crash will
usually pick up where it left off.
