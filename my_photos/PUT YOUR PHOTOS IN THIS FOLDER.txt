Drop 20 to 50 photos of ONE person into this folder.

Good photos:
  - Sharp and in focus
  - A mix of close-up face, waist-up, and full body
  - Different outfits, lighting, and backgrounds
  - Just the one person in frame

Avoid:
  - Blurry or heavily filtered shots
  - Sunglasses or anything covering the face in most of them
  - Group photos
  - Near-identical duplicates

iPhone .HEIC photos are fine. JPG, PNG, and WEBP all work too.

You can delete this text file once you have added your photos,
or just leave it. It gets ignored either way.
